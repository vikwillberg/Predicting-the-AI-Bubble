Anthropic is about to go public at a reported $1.5 trillion. So is AI a bubble, and when does it pop?

I built a model to find out. It learns what the year before a crash looks like from 67 assets since 1927, then scores a basket of 20 AI leaders.

The answer: a 42% chance the basket falls 40%+ from its Oct 6 peak within a year. 63% within two.

Why so high? 15 of the 20 are already in bear markets while Microsoft and NVIDIA hold up. That's the 2000 pattern. Last quarter, Big Tech spent $1.16 on capex for every $1 of cash its businesses brought in. And to justify $1.5T, Anthropic needs about $800B of revenue by 2036. That's more than Amazon makes today.

The part that surprised me: on Oct 2, Sornette's bubble model said the AI basket had hit its critical point. It peaked 4 days later.

Backtest check: at the Nasdaq's 2000 peak, the same model said 29%, about 2x the normal rate at the time.

42% is a risk, not a certainty.
