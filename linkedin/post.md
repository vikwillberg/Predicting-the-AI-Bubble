Anthropic is about to list at up to $1.5 trillion. Everyone's asking: is AI a bubble, and when does it pop?

So I built a model to answer it. ICARUS learns what the year before a crash looks like from 67 assets since 1927, from the dot-com bust to bitcoin, and scores today's AI leaders.

The answer: 42% odds the AI-20 falls 40%+ from its Oct 6 peak within a year, 63% within two.

Why so high? 15 of 20 AI leaders are already in bear markets while Microsoft and NVIDIA hold up. That's the 2000 pattern. And Big Tech now spends $1.16 on capex for every $1 of cash its businesses bring in.

At $1.5T, Anthropic needs ~$802B of revenue by 2036. Possible, but it's a big ask.

The part that surprised me: Sornette's bubble clock, fitted 4 days before the top, put the critical point at Oct 2. The top came Oct 6.

42% isn't a sell signal. It's a risk, not a certainty.
