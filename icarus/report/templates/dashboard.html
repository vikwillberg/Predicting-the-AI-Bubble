What the model can and cannot say

  • A probability is not a date. A 40% chance in 12 months still means 60% that it does not happen.
  • The AI-20 is built from today's AI leaders, so its history is flattered by hindsight. The model is trained on 60+ other assets and only scores the AI-20.
  • The training panel holds only assets that still trade (no Pets.com or Lucent), which makes single-stock crashes look rarer than they were.
  • Anthropic and OpenAI figures come from press coverage of filings and funding rounds, each with a source and a confidence level in config/private_marks.yaml.
  • This is a research project, not investment advice.
